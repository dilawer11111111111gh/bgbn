The heaviest boat in Puddleport lasted exactly two seconds.
Burl built it for the big race. Solid iron, nose to tail.
"Unsinkable," he said.
His rival Pip used the same iron… but she bent it hollow, like a giant soup bowl.
The whole dock laughed at her.
Race day. Burl launched first… blub. Straight to the bottom.
Pip's bowl? It floated. And won.
You see, a boat floats when it pushes away more water than it weighs.
Hollow means more water pushed, with the same iron.
That's buoyancy.
And Burl? He still races. Underwater.
