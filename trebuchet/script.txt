The biggest catapult in the kingdom couldn't reach the castle. A box of rocks did. King Bramblewick marches his army to the walls of Grimhold Castle. He brings the mightiest catapult ever built. Ten soldiers pull the rope. Twenty. Thirty. Snap! The rock flies… and lands on the king's tent. An old carpenter builds something strange: a long beam, a sling on one end… and a giant box of rocks on the other. He pulls one tiny pin. The box drops. The beam whips. The rock flies over the wall, over the castle, and into the castle's soup. Here's why. Ropes can only pull as hard as people can. But a falling weight never gets tired. The heavy box drops a short way, and the long arm turns that drop into a giant throw. That's a counterweight trebuchet. The king never lost a battle again. He did lose his tent.
