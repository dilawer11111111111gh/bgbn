# Coaster video: Cartoon Animator 5 ki shot list

Har shot ke liye Cartoon Animator 5 (CA5) mein character ki acting banayein. Phir use **transparent PNG sequence** export karke zip mein bhejein. 3D duniya, track, cart, camera aur roshni main banata hoon. Aap ke characters usi duniya mein khade kar dunga.

## Pehle ek baar: characters banayein

| Naam | Look (hamari video jaisa) |
|---|---|
| HERO (coaster ka maalik) | Bada safed sar, narangi helmet, bhure baal, hari jacket, safed shirt, patli kaali taangein |
| RIVAL | Bada safed sar, kaali top hat (peeli patti), jamni coat, safed shirt, peeli tie, kaali moonchh |
| DUMMY | Peela gol jism, safed sar, seedha chehra (sirf shot 5 mein) |
| TOWN 1, TOWN 2 | Kisan (dungree, tinke ki topi) aur bachi (check wali frock, laal patti) |

Style ye rahe: sar bada aur gol, moti outline, jism bean jaisa, aur bazu aur taangein rubber ki tarah mudne wali.

## Export ki settings (har shot ke liye)

- **Format:** PNG Sequence, **Transparent background (Alpha) ON**
- **FPS:** 30
- **Size:** 1080 × 1920 (vertical), character frame ke beech mein
- **Lambai:** neeche diye gaye shot ke start se end tak
- **Folder ka naam:** shot ka code, jaise `s01_hammer`. Files `0001.png`, `0002.png` ...
- **Awaaz:** reference video ki audio CA5 timeline mein daal lein taake har action sahi lafz par ho. Export mein audio ki zaroorat nahi.

## Kaise bhejein

1. Har shot ka folder (jaise `s01_hammer`) bana kar sab ko ek zip mein daal dein.
2. Zip yahan chat mein upload kar dein.
3. Main har folder ko us ke waqt par 3D scene mein laga dunga. Character camera ki taraf dekhega aur saaye ke saath zameen par khada hoga. Phir poori video render karke muqable ke saath bhejunga.

**Shuru kahan se karein:** pehle sirf `s01_hammer` bana kar bhej dein. Wo lagakar dikhata hoon, taake baaqi shots se pehle pata chal jaye ke sab theek jur raha hai.
